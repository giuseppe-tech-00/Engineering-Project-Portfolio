About me

I'm an engineer with years of experience building systems that bridge hardware and software — from mechanical assemblies and embedded control loops to AI-driven decision pipelines and space-grade structures.

My work lives at the intersection of multiple disciplines. I believe the most interesting engineering problems are the ones that don't fit neatly into a single field. I've worked on autonomous systems, structural mechanics, simulation environments, and more — often all within the same project.

This portfolio is a living record of that journey: the prototypes, the papers, the late-night debugging sessions, and the collaborators who made it all possible.
